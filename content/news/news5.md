Honda has reinforced its electric motorcycle design ambition with a major international award. The **Honda WN7** won **Gold** in Product Design at the **iF DESIGN AWARD 2026**, one of the world's most established design competitions. It is Honda's first Gold distinction in iF history.

WN7 is Honda's first electric naked model in the **FUN** category and was developed around the "Become the Wind" concept. Performance claims are strong: up to **50 kW** output (600cc-class equivalent) and **100 Nm** torque (1000cc-class equivalent). Its **frameless architecture**, where the battery case also functions as a structural element, supports both weight reduction and packaging flexibility. Signature styling highlights include a horizontal-bar headlamp and black-and-gold detailing.

The iF jury noted that WN7 successfully merges classic motorcycle DNA with an electric identity in a way that feels coherent rather than forced.

Honda R&D Design Center's Toshinobu Minami described the recognition as validation of new values for the electrification era, centered on the joy of free riding.
