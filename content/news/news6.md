CFMOTO has partnered with a major name to strengthen its adventure positioning. The China-based motorcycle brand announced Spanish **Extreme Enduro** rider **Mario Roman Serrano** as an official brand ambassador.

Serrano, known as a racer, trainer, and content creator, has earned top results in premier off-road events such as **Red Bull Erzbergrodeo** and **Red Bull Romaniacs**. He has also reached podium-level success in Hard Enduro and led Spanish Enduro rankings. His technical riding style and creative line choices earned him the nickname **"The Magician."**

As part of the collaboration, Mario will put CFMOTO's **MT series** at the center of his adventure rides and race-related projects. His **MR74 Academy** rider training program will also connect with CFMOTO's global activities. The brand aims to position MT models not only as motorcycles but as tools that help riders expand their limits.
