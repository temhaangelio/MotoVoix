Triumph Motorcycles is celebrating the 15th anniversary of the world's most stylish motorcycle event with a single special machine. The **Speed Twin 1200 Cafe Racer DGR Edition** was built from scratch for The Distinguished Gentleman's Ride 2026 and will be awarded to this year's **Gentlefolk Prize** winner.

The most striking detail is the seat: crafted from handwoven **Harris Tweed®** in Scotland's Outer Hebrides and delivered with a certificate of authenticity. The body also features a **"15 Years of Dapper"** anniversary graphic and a dedicated number plate panel. Triumph has supported DGR for years as the event's official motorcycle partner.

The **Distinguished Gentleman's Ride** is held every year to raise funds for men's health and prostate cancer research worldwide. In 2025, 127,000 riders from 108 countries took part and raised **$7.6 million**. The 2026 ride takes place on **May 17**, and registrations are now open.
