As Vespa approaches its 80th anniversary, it has updated the iconic Primavera with meaningful upgrades in both safety and technology. The **2026 Vespa Primavera** keeps its classic Italian styling while delivering a more contemporary riding experience.

The standout update this year is the braking system. On 125cc and 150cc versions, the rear drum brake is replaced by a **220mm steel disc brake**. A dual-piston caliper and linked **ABS** support safer urban stopping performance. The **keyless start** system, now standard on all versions except the 50cc, also controls the seat lock and includes a Bike Finder function.

A new **full LCD instrument panel** is now available across the range, while the upper-trim Primavera Tech features a **5-inch TFT display** and **Vespa MIA** smartphone connectivity. Design changes include new **five-spoke 12-inch wheels**, a revised front fender badge, and a practical **under-handlebar bag hook**. Engine options remain **50cc, 125cc, and 150cc** gasoline, plus removable-battery electric variants.

One electric model is limited to **45 km/h** in moped configuration, while the other offers full performance. European pricing starts at **EUR 4,250** for the 50cc, **EUR 5,350** for the 125cc, and **EUR 5,450** for the 150cc. With new paint options such as **Beige Avvolgente** and **Giallo Disinvolto**, the Primavera keeps standing out in urban traffic.
